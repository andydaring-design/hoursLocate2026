Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how locateHours2026 ("the App") handles information when you use the App with Google Sheets.

The App is a Google Apps Script designed to help users manage working-time periods within a Google Spreadsheet.

2. Information the App accesses

The App accesses only the Google Spreadsheet in which it is installed and used.

The App may read and modify spreadsheet data necessary to perform its functions, including:

Reading the selected cell and spreadsheet rows
Reading existing cell values
Adding rows to the spreadsheet
Modifying cell contents and formatting
Adjusting row heights
Moving the active cell

The App requests the following Google authorization scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly

This permission allows the App to access and modify the current spreadsheet only.

3. Information the App does not collect

The App does not:

Collect or store personal information on external servers
Sell or share user information
Send spreadsheet contents to the developer
Access the user's Gmail, Google Drive files, Calendar, Contacts, or other Google services
Track users across websites or services
Use spreadsheet information for advertising
Use spreadsheet information for profiling users

All spreadsheet processing performed by the App takes place within the user's Google Sheets environment.

4. Data storage

The App does not maintain an external database or server-side storage of user spreadsheet information.

Any information entered into or modified within the spreadsheet remains subject to Google's storage and privacy policies.

5. Third-party services

The App uses Google Apps Script and Google Sheets to provide its functionality.

The App does not transfer user spreadsheet data to any third-party service.

6. Data security

Because the App does not transmit or store spreadsheet information on external servers, the App itself does not maintain a separate copy of users' spreadsheet data.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for their Google account and spreadsheet, including controlling who has access to the spreadsheet.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the App changes or if necessary to comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

The latest version will always be made available at this webpage.
